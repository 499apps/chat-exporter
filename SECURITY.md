# Security policy

Please report security or privacy issues privately by email to **support@499apps.com** rather than in a public issue. Include the extension version, your browser version, the chat site and steps to reproduce. We aim to reply within a few days and will credit you in the fix if you like.

Convo Keeper runs entirely on your device: no servers, no accounts, no network calls. Reports about anything that could leak conversation content are especially welcome.
