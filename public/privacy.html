Convo Keeper Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026

Convo Keeper exports the conversation open in your current browser tab to a file on your computer. It works entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Convo Keeper has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no telemetry. It makes no network requests; its content security policy blocks them (connect-src 'none').

What the extension reads

Only when you click an export button, it reads the conversation shown in the active tab on ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok. The text is turned into a Markdown, JSON or printable file and saved where you choose. For PDF, the conversation is held briefly in the browser's in-memory session storage so the print page can display it, then deleted.

Sharing

We never sell, transfer or share data, because none ever leaves your device.

Permissions

  • activeTab: access to the current tab only after you click the toolbar button.
  • scripting: run the bundled reader in that tab to collect the visible messages.
  • storage: hand the conversation to the local print page for PDF export (session storage, cleared after use).

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be published with new versions in the project repository. Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
